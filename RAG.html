The Mechanics of RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation bridges proprietary facts to static LLM weights. Walk through chunking, vector indexing, dense retrieval, and augmented prompting below.

Phase 1

Ingestion, Chunking & Dense Embeddings

Raw documents are too large for token limits and dense search. We split documents into smaller chunks (with overlap to preserve context) and convert them into mathematical vectors (embeddings) capturing semantic topics.

120
Phase 2

Query Vectorization & Cosine Similarity Search

When a user asks a question, we vectorize their query with the same embedding model and calculate the Cosine Similarity against our indexed chunks to find the most semantically relevant information.

Phase 3

Prompt Augmentation & Grounded Response

We inject the retrieved factual context directly into the LLM's system prompt. Compare a standard generic LLM response (which lacks real-time knowledge and may hallucinate) against the RAG-grounded response.
